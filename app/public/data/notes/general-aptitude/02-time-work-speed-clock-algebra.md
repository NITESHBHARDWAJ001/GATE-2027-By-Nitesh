## 1. Time & Work

- **Rate = 1/time (work per day).** A alone a din, B alone b din ⇒ saath me **ab/(a+b)** din.
- **Total work = LCM lekar units** (easy): A 12 din, B 18 din ⇒ work 36; A 3/din, B 2/din ⇒ 5/din ⇒ 7.2 din.
- **Work ∝ men × days × hours** (M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂).
- **Pipes & cisterns:** inlet +, outlet −.
- **Efficiency ratio ∝ 1/time.**
**Example:** A 10 din, B 15 din: saath = 150/25 = **6 din**.
**Example:** A, B saath 8 din, A akela 12 din ⇒ B: 1/8 − 1/12 = 1/24 ⇒ **24 din**.
**Example:** Tap 6 ghante bharta, outlet 10 ghante khali: saath 1/6 − 1/10 = 1/15 ⇒ **15 ghante**.
**Example:** 10 men 15 din me kaam; 5 din baad 5 aur men: pehle 5 din me 10·5 = 50 man-day (total 150), bache 100, 15 men ⇒ 6.67 din.

## 2. Speed, Time, Distance

- **Distance = Speed × Time.** km/h → m/s: × 5/18; m/s → km/h: × 18/5. 36 km/h = 10 m/s.
- **Average speed = total distance/total time.** Equal distances: 2ab/(a+b).
- **Relative speed:** same dir **u − v**; opposite **u + v**.
- **Train problems:** train ka length + platform/other train ka length cross karne me. Pole/man cross: sirf apni length.
- **Boat & stream:** downstream = b + s; upstream = b − s. b = (D + U)/2; s = (D − U)/2.
- **Time ratio inverse speed ratio.** Distance same: speed ratio a:b ⇒ time b:a.
- **Meeting:** dono ek doosre ki taraf, d/(u+v).
- **Circular track:** same direction meet: L/(u−v); opposite: L/(u+v).
**Example:** 150 m train 54 km/h (15 m/s) 150 m platform: 300/15 = **20 s**.
**Example:** Do trains 60, 40 km/h opposite; lengths 100, 150 m: 250 m at 100 km/h = 250/27.78 = **9 s**.
**Example:** Boat 10 km/h, stream 2: 24 km down = 2 ghante; up = 3 ghante ⇒ round trip 5 ghante; avg = 48/5 = 9.6.
**Example:** 60 km/h se jaana, 40 km/h aana ⇒ avg 48.

## 3. Clock problems
- **Minute hand 6°/min; hour hand 0.5°/min; relative 5.5°/min.**
- **Angle at h:m = |30h − 5.5m|** (agar > 180 to 360 se minus).
- **Hands coincide** har **65 5/11 min**; 12 ghante me 11 baar; din me 22 baar. Opposite: 11 baar/12 h; right angle 22 baar/12 h (44/day).
- **Fast/slow clock:** ghadi jitni gain/lose.
**Example:** 3:00 pe angle 90°. 7:20 pe: |210 − 110| = **100°**. 3:40: |90 − 220| = 130°.
**Example:** 4:00 ke baad kab hands overlap: 4 × 60/11 = 21.8 min ⇒ 4:21 9/11.

## 4. Algebra
### Quadratic ax² + bx + c = 0
- **Roots x = (−b ± √D)/2a; D = b² − 4ac.** D > 0: 2 real; = 0: equal; < 0: complex.
- **Sum = −b/a; product = c/a.** Roots ka function: α² + β² = (α+β)² − 2αβ.
- **Max/min at x = −b/2a.** a > 0 min; value c − b²/4a.
### Identities
(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; a² − b² = (a−b)(a+b); a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a² − ab + b²); (a+b+c)² …
**Example:** x² − 5x + 6 = 0 ⇒ 2, 3. Roots sum 5, product 6. x² + 4x + 5: D = −4 < 0 ⇒ complex.
**Example:** min of x² − 6x + 10 = 1 (x=3). x + 1/x ≥ 2 (x > 0).
**Example:** Polynomial degree n ke ≤ n roots; **remainder theorem:** p(x)/(x − a) ka remainder p(a).
### Functions
Domain/range; **one-one, onto**; f(g(x)) composition; even/odd. **Linear equations ke do variables** simultaneous solve; **inequalities**: negative se multiply par sign palto.
**Example:** f(x) = 2x + 3, f(f(2)) = f(7) = 17.
**Word problems (age):** "5 saal pehle baap ki age beta se 4 guna thi" ⇒ equation banao.

## 5. Series aur Number series
- **AP:** aₙ = a + (n−1)d; **Sₙ = n/2(2a + (n−1)d) = n(a + l)/2.**
- **GP:** aₙ = arⁿ⁻¹; **Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1); S∞ = a/(1 − r), |r| < 1.**
- **Σn = n(n+1)/2, Σn² = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6, Σn³ = (Σn)².**
- **Number series patterns:** difference, ratio, squares/cubes, primes, alternating, Fibonacci-type, "×2 + 1".
**Example:** 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ? ⇒ n² + 1 ⇒ **37**.
**Example:** 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ? ⇒ 13. 2, 6, 12, 20, 30 ⇒ n(n+1) ⇒ 42.
**Example:** 1 + 2 + … + 100 = 5050. Multiples of 3 ka sum ≤ 100: 3(33·34/2) = 1683.

## 6. Permutation, Combination, Probability
- **Counting:** multiplication (AND), addition (OR).
- **nPr = n!/(n−r)!; nCr = n!/(r!(n−r)!).** nCr = nC(n−r).
- **Repeating letters arrangement: n!/(p! q!…).** **Circular arrangement: (n − 1)!.**
- **Handshakes n(n−1)/2.** **Diagonals n(n−3)/2.**
- **At least one:** complement.
- **Probability = favourable/total** (detail EM notes me).
**Example:** MISSISSIPPI: 11!/(4!4!2!) = 34650. **INDIA** letters ke arrangements: 5!/2! = 60.
**Example:** 5 log round table: 4! = 24. **Committee 3 of 8:** 56.
**Example:** 6 boys 4 girls, committee 3 with ≥ 1 girl: C(10,3) − C(6,3) = 120 − 20 = 100.
**Example:** Do dice sum 8: 5/36.

## 7. Venn Diagram / Sets
- **|A ∪ B| = |A| + |B| − |A ∩ B|.** 3 sets: Σ − Σ + |A∩B∩C|.
**Example:** 100 students me 60 cricket, 50 football, 20 dono ⇒ kam se kam ek: 90; koi nahi: 10.
**Example:** 1–100 me 2 ya 3 se divisible: 50 + 33 − 16 = 67.
