## 1. Subject–Verb Agreement (sabse zyada poocha)
- Singular subject ⇒ singular verb (is/has/goes); plural ⇒ plural.
- **Each, every, either, neither, one of the + plural noun, everyone, nobody ⇒ singular.** "One of the boys **is** absent."
- **Neither…nor / either…or:** verb **nearest subject** ke saath. "Neither Ram nor his friends **are** coming."
- **Ek hi sense me do noun (bread and butter is)** singular; **collective noun (team, committee)** singular jab ek unit.
- **Uncountable nouns (information, advice, furniture, equipment, news, luggage, knowledge)** singular, "an information" galat.
- **Plural-looking singular:** mathematics, physics, news, economics.
- **Subject aur verb ke beech ke phrase ignore:** "The **list** of items **is** long."
- **Inversion:** "There **are** many reasons."
**Example:** ❌ "Each of the students have passed." ✅ **has**.
**Example:** ❌ "The data are/is": data plural formally (**data are**), par GATE me option se dekho.

## 2. Tenses
| Tense | Use |
|---|---|
| Simple present | Habit/truth: "Water boils at 100°C." |
| Present continuous | Abhi chal raha |
| Present perfect (has/have + V3) | Past action jiska abhi ka asar; "since/for" |
| Past perfect (had + V3) | Do past actions me pehla |
| Simple past | Nishchit past (yesterday, ago) |
| Future | will/shall |

- **Since + point (2010), for + period (5 years).**
- **"Yesterday/ago/last year" ke saath present perfect nahi.** ❌ "I have seen him yesterday."
- **If clauses:** If + present, will; If + past, would; **If + had + V3, would have + V3.**
- **"Wish/as if" ke baad past subjunctive:** "I wish I **were** …".
- **Time/condition clause me will nahi:** "If it **rains** tomorrow", "when he **comes**".
**Example:** ❌ "If I would have known, I would have come." ✅ "If I **had** known…".
**Example:** ❌ "He is working here since 2010." ✅ "has been working".

## 3. Articles (a, an, the)
- **a + consonant sound**, **an + vowel sound** (an hour, a university, an MBA, a one-eyed).
- **the:** specific/unique (the sun, the Ganga, the Himalayas, the best), superlative se pehle.
- **No article:** general plural/uncountable, languages, meals, sports; "go to school/bed/church" (purpose).
**Example:** "He is **an** honest man." "She plays **the** violin/ plays cricket."

## 4. Prepositions
- **Time:** at (exact time: at 5), on (day/date), in (month/year/part of day: in the morning; **at night**).
- **Place:** at (point), in (enclosed area), on (surface).
- **Common pairs:** **good at, interested in, capable of, afraid of, depend on, agree with (person)/to (proposal)/on (issue), different from, superior/inferior to, prefer X to Y, married to, accuse of, congratulate on, insist on, result in / result from, complain about/of, pay attention to, aware of, proud of, jealous of, fond of, rich in, blind to, responsible for, suffer from.**
- **Between (2), among (3+).** **Since/for.** **By (deadline/agent), until.**
**Example:** ❌ "He is senior than me." ✅ "senior **to** me". ❌ "prefer tea than coffee" ✅ "**to** coffee".

## 5. Comparatives aur Superlatives
- Short adj: taller, tallest; long adj: more/most beautiful. Irregular: good–better–best, bad–worse–worst, far–farther/further, little–less–least, many/much–more–most.
- **Double comparative galat:** ❌ "more better".
- **Comparison of same kind:** ❌ "The population of Delhi is more than Mumbai." ✅ "…**that of** Mumbai."
- **Latin comparatives (to):** senior, junior, superior, inferior, prior, prefer + **to**.
- **"Than" ke saath comparative; "of/in" superlative ke saath.** "He is the **tallest** **of** all boys."
- **as … as (positive).** **"No sooner … than", "Hardly … when", "Scarcely … when".**
- **The + comparative … the + comparative:** "The more, the merrier."
**Example:** ❌ "He is the most cleverest." ✅ "the cleverest".

## 6. Other frequent grammar rules
- **Pronoun agreement:** one … one's; everyone … his/her/their (their accepted).
- **Who/whom:** subject who, object whom. **Whose** possession.
- **Modifiers:** dangling modifier — "Walking down the street, the tree fell" ❌.
- **Parallel structure:** "She likes swimming, reading, and to dance" ❌ ⇒ "dancing".
- **Fewer (countable) vs less (uncountable); many vs much; number vs amount.**
- **Its (possessive) vs it's (it is); their/there/they're; then/than; affect (verb)/effect (noun).**
- **Redundancy:** "return back", "reverse back", "repeat again" galat.
- **Double negatives:** ❌ "He didn't do nothing."
- **Participle:** "Having finished work, he left."
**Example:** ❌ "The number of students **are** increasing." ✅ "**is**" (the number of = singular; "a number of" = plural).

## 7. Most Appropriate Word / Fill in the blank / Word pairs
Strategy:
1. **Blank ka role pehchano** (noun/verb/adj) aur context ki tone (positive/negative).
2. **Signal words:** but, although, however ⇒ contrast; therefore, hence ⇒ result; and ⇒ similar.
3. **Elimination:** grammar galat options hatao, phir meaning.
4. **Word pairs (analogy):** relation same: **synonym, antonym, part–whole, tool–user, degree (warm : hot), cause–effect.**
**Example:** "Despite her _____ appearance, she was quite strong." ⇒ **frail** (contrast).
**Example:** Word pair *Introvert : Extrovert :: Reticent : Talkative* (antonym pair).
**Example:** "The committee was **unanimous** in its decision" (sabhi sehmat).

## 11. Quick Revision
- Each/every/neither/one of ⇒ singular; nearest subject rule for neither…nor.
- Since (point) / for (period); if + had V3 ⇒ would have V3.
- a/an by sound; prefer X **to** Y; senior/superior **to**.
- Double comparative galat; compare like with like ("that of").
- Signal words: but/although ⇒ contrast.
- Vocabulary: root + context.
