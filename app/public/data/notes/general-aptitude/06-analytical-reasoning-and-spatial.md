## Part A — Analytical / Logical Reasoning

### 1. Seating arrangement
- **Linear:** left/right speaker ke hisaab se (facing north vs facing audience ka dhyan).
- **Circular:** **facing centre** ⇒ apne **left = clockwise** direction, right = anticlockwise; **facing outward** ⇒ ulta. **n log circular me opposite = n/2 door.**
- **Method:** diagram/positions 1..n, definite clues pehle (X is exactly between A and B), phir conditional; **cases banao aur contradiction pe hatao.**
- **Total circular arrangements (n − 1)!**.
**Example:** 5 log A–E line me: C sabse left, B, A ke left me, D, E ke beech me nahi ⇒ definite clue se C fix, phir baaki cases likho; jo case contradiction de use hatao.

### 2. Direction sense
- **N, E, S, W; NE, etc.** Right turn: N→E→S→W (clockwise); left ulta.
- **Displacement = √(Δx² + Δy²)** (Pythagoras); direction = final position ka origin se angle.
**Example:** 5 km North, 5 km East, 5 km South: origin se **5 km East**. 3 N + 4 E: displacement **5 km**, direction NE-ish (N-E quadrant).
**Example:** Facing North, right turn, right turn ⇒ **South**. Shadow: morning me sun east ⇒ shadow west.

### 3. Family relationships & Age
- **Symbols:** male □, female ○; = married; vertical = generation.
- **Common terms:** father's father = grandfather; **mother's brother = maternal uncle (mama)**; father's sister = bua; sister's husband = brother-in-law; **son of my father's only sibling** = cousin.
- **"Pointing to a man, X says: he is the son of my mother's only son."** Mother's only son = X khud ⇒ **son**.
- **Age problems:** equations: "5 saal baad …", "ratio 3:4" ⇒ variables x, y.
**Example:** B, A ki maa hai; C, B ki behen; D, C ka beta ⇒ D aur A **maternal cousins** hain (maa ki behen ka beta).
**Example:** Baap ki age beta se 4 guna; 5 saal baad 3 guna: F = 4S, F + 5 = 3(S + 5) ⇒ 4S + 5 = 3S + 15 ⇒ S = 10, F = 40.

### 4. Coding–Decoding & code words
- **Letter shift:** CAT → DBU (+1). **Position value:** A = 1 … Z = 26; opposite letter (A↔Z, sum 27).
- **Reverse alphabet, word ka reverse, vowel/consonant substitution.**
- **Word codes:** do-teen sentences me common word ka code common code se nikalo.
**Example:** "MANGO" → "NBOHP" (+1 shift): "APPLE" → "BQQMF".
**Example:** "ram is good = 123; good boy = 345; ram boy = 156?" common word se decode.

### 5. Analogy, Odd one out, Number relations
- **Analogy relations:** synonym, opposite, part–whole, tool–work, cause–effect, category, degree.
- **Odd one out:** category ya property alag; numbers me prime/square/cube mix.
- **Number relation:** **A : B :: C : ?** pattern (square, cube, ×2+1).
**Example:** 3 : 9 :: 4 : **16** (square). 2 : 8 :: 3 : **27** (cube). Pehle pattern verify karo, phir apply.
**Example:** Odd one: 4, 9, 16, 20, 25 ⇒ **20** (baaki perfect squares).

### 6. Logical reasoning (puzzles / conditional)
- **Truth-teller / liar:** contradiction check karke assignment.
- **Conditionals:** contrapositive.
- **Selection ("Exactly one of A, B, C"), scheduling**: table banao.
**Example:** A: "B jhoot bolta hai." B: "A aur B dono jhooth bolte hain." Agar B sach ⇒ B jhoothi (contradiction); to B liar ⇒ uska statement false ⇒ "dono liar" false ⇒ A truthful ⇒ A ka statement sach (B liar) ✓. Answer: **A truthful, B liar.**

### 7. Series (letters/numbers)
- **Number:** difference, ratio, squares, alternate interleaved series.
- **Letter:** position values, alternate letters.
**Example:** B, E, H, K, ? ⇒ +3 ⇒ **N**. 2, 6, 18, 54 ⇒ ×3.

## Part B — Spatial Aptitude

### 1. Paper folding & cutting
- **Symmetry:** fold line par cut ⇒ **mirror copy** (2 holes for one fold, 4 for two folds).
- **Unfold in reverse order**; har unfolding me hole fold line ke opposite side reflect.
**Example:** Square 2 baar fold (half, half), corner me 1 hole ⇒ unfold: **4 holes symmetrically**.

### 2. Mirror image & Water image
- **Mirror (vertical mirror, right side):** left–right swap (text reverse); **horizontal mirror:** top–bottom swap.
- **Clock in mirror:** time = 11:60 − t (12:00 − t).
**Example:** Clock 3:15 mirror ⇒ 8:45.
**Example:** Mirror image of "b" (mirror to its right) ⇒ "d".

### 3. Rotation / reflection of figures
- 90° clockwise: top → right. Symmetry order (square 4-fold, rectangle 2-fold, equilateral triangle 3, circle ∞).
- **Track ek reference feature (marked corner/arrow)**.

### 4. 3D structures, cubes/dice
- **Cube unfolded (net):** 11 valid nets; **net me opposite faces:** ek hi row/column me beech me ek square chhodkar wale squares opposite hote hain.
- **Dice:** opposite faces sum 7 (standard). **Three visible faces se opposite face** nikalo (common face rule).
- **Counting cubes in stacked structure:** layers ko top view + column height se count; **hidden cubes ke liye support.**
**Example:** Dice ke faces 1, 2, 3 visible corner par; opposite: 6, 5, 4.
**Example:** 3×3×3 me se center cube nikalo: 26 cubes, painted faces surface ka.

### 5. Assembling pieces / 2D patterns
- **Pieces ko rotate/flip karke bade shape me fit;** **corner pieces, edges pehle**; area check: pieces ka total = target area.
- **Pattern completion:** repeating motif ki symmetry (translation/rotation/reflection).
- **Counting shapes:** triangles/squares in figure: **systematically size-wise gino.** n×n grid me squares = Σk² (2×2: 5; 3×3: 14; 4×4: 30); rectangles = [n(n+1)/2]² (3×3: 36).
**Example:** 2×2 grid squares 4 + 1 = 5; rectangles 9.
