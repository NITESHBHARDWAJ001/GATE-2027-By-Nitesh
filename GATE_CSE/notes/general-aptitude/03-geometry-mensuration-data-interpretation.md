## 1. Basic Geometry

- **Angles:** complementary 90°, supplementary 180°. Triangle angles sum 180°; **exterior angle = sum of two opposite interior.** **Polygon interior angle sum = (n − 2)·180°**; regular polygon each = (n−2)180/n.
- **Parallel lines + transversal:** corresponding, alternate equal; co-interior supplementary.
- **Similar triangles:** angles equal ⇒ sides proportional; **area ratio = (side ratio)²**.
- **Pythagoras:** a² + b² = c². Triples: **3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25**.
- **Triangle inequality:** har side < sum of other two, > their difference.
- **Special triangles:** 30-60-90 sides 1 : √3 : 2; 45-45-90: 1 : 1 : √2.
- **Centroid** medians ka meet (2:1). **Circumcentre** perpendicular bisectors; **incentre** angle bisectors.
- **Diagonal of rectangle/square:** √(l²+b²); square a√2.
**Example:** Right triangle legs 6, 8 ⇒ hypotenuse 10, area 24.
**Example:** Regular hexagon each angle 120°; octagon 135°.
**Example:** Do similar triangles sides 3:5 ⇒ areas 9:25.

## 4. Coordinate Geometry (Cartesian)

- **Distance:** √((x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²). **Midpoint:** ((x₁+x₂)/2, (y₁+y₂)/2). **Section formula (m:n):** (mx₂ + nx₁)/(m+n).
- **Slope m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁);** parallel m₁ = m₂; perpendicular m₁m₂ = −1.
- **Line:** y = mx + c; ax + by + c = 0. **Point–line distance |ax₀ + by₀ + c|/√(a² + b²).**
- **Circle:** (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r².
- **Triangle area** = ½|x₁(y₂−y₃) + x₂(y₃−y₁) + x₃(y₁−y₂)|. Collinear ⟺ area 0.
- **Quadrants:** I (+,+), II (−,+), III (−,−), IV (+,−). Reflection x-axis: (x, −y); y-axis: (−x, y); origin: (−x, −y); y = x: (y, x).
**Example:** (0,0) se (3,4) distance 5. Midpoint (1,2),(5,6) = (3,4).
**Example:** (1,1), (2,3), (3,5): area = ½|1(3−5) + 2(5−1) + 3(1−3)| = ½|−2 + 8 − 6| = 0 ⇒ collinear.
**Example:** Line y = 2x + 1 ke perpendicular slope −1/2.

## 5. Data Interpretation (DI)

GATE DI: **table, bar graph, line graph, pie chart, mixed** — calculations approximate karo, options door door hote hain.

- **Pie chart:** total 360° = 100%; **x% = 3.6x°.** Sector angle se quantity: (angle/360) × total.
- **Bar/line:** scale dekho (y-axis start 0 hai?). **Growth% = (new − old)/old × 100.** Highest/lowest growth: **slope** compare.
- **Table:** row/column totals, ratios; **average, percentage share.**
- **CAGR-type:** (final/initial)^(1/n) − 1.
- **Approximation techniques:** round numbers, fractions ↔ percent (chapter 1 table).
- **Traps:** units (lakh/crore, thousand), "percentage points" vs "percent", truncated axis, cumulative vs yearly.
**Example:** Pie me A = 90° ⇒ 25%. Total sales 2 lakh ⇒ A = 50,000.
**Example:** Table: 2020 = 200, 2021 = 250: growth 25%. Agar 2022 = 300: growth 20% (slower).
**Example:** Bar chart "kis saal me sabse zyada vridhi" — consecutive differences.

## 6. Statistics (basic, aptitude level)
- **Mean, median (beech ka), mode (sabse zyada).** Median: sorted; even n ⇒ dono beech ka avg. **Mean > median > mode right-skewed.**
- **Range = max − min. Variance/SD:** spread. Data me constant add ⇒ mean shift, SD same; scale ⇒ dono scale.
- **Weighted mean.**
**Example:** 2, 3, 5, 5, 10: mean 5, median 5, mode 5. 1, 2, 3, 4: median 2.5.

## 7. Maps, Contour plots, Cubes/dice
- **Map:** scale (1 cm = x km); distance = map distance × scale; direction N up.
- **Contour plot:** same value lines; **dense lines = steep**; **closed loop = peak/valley**; contour kabhi cross nahi.
- **Dice:** opposite faces sum 7 (standard: 1-6, 2-5, 3-4). Cube ke 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.
**Example:** Map scale 1 cm : 5 km; 4.5 cm ⇒ 22.5 km.

## Extra: Misc quant (image, graph, number-representation, shortest path)
- **Digital image:** image = pixel grid; **size = rows × cols × bits/pixel**. 100×100, 8-bit ⇒ 10,000 bytes. **Grayscale 8-bit: 256 levels.** RGB 24-bit.
- **Graph colouring (aptitude level):** adjacent regions alag colour; **map 4-colour theorem**; **triangle (K₃) needs 3**, bipartite/even cycle 2, odd cycle 3, Kₙ needs n.
- **Number representation:** binary 101 = 5; base-b conversion: repeated division; **hex 2A = 42**; place value.
- **Shortest path (grid/graph):** lattice path right/up from (0,0) to (m,n): **C(m+n, m)**; weighted graph: chhote paths compare karo (Dijkstra idea); BFS for equal weights.
