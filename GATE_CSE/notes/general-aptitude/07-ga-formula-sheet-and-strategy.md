## 1. One-page formula sheet

**Arithmetic**
- % change = Δ/old × 100; successive: a + b + ab/100; profit% on CP.
- Same SP x% profit & loss ⇒ loss x²/100 %. Successive discount a + b − ab/100.
- CI: P(1 + r/100)ⁿ; CI − SI (2 yr) = P(r/100)².
- Avg speed (equal dist) 2ab/(a+b). Alligation (d − m):(m − c).
- HCF × LCM = product; factors Π(a+1); trailing zeros n! Σ⌊n/5ᵏ⌋.

**Time-work-speed**
- A, B together ab/(a+b). Work ∝ M·D·H.
- km/h × 5/18 = m/s. Relative speed: same − , opposite +. Train: length sum ÷ relative speed.
- Boat: b = (D+U)/2; s = (D−U)/2.
- Clock: angle |30h − 5.5m|; overlap every 65 5/11 min.

**Algebra & counting**
- Quadratic: sum −b/a, product c/a, D = b² − 4ac.
- AP Sₙ = n/2(a + l); GP S∞ = a/(1−r); Σn, Σn², Σn³.
- nPr, nCr; circular (n−1)!; repeated n!/(p!q!); |A∪B| = |A| + |B| − |A∩B|.

**Geometry**
- Circle πr², 2πr; triangle ½bh; equilateral √3a²/4; Pythagoras triples 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
- Cube 6a², a³; cylinder πr²h; cone ⅓πr²h; sphere 4/3πr³, 4πr².
- Similar ⇒ area ratio = (side ratio)². Polygon angle sum (n−2)180.
- Distance √(Δx²+Δy²); slope m; perpendicular m₁m₂ = −1.

**Verbal & reasoning**
- Each/every/neither ⇒ singular; since/for; prefer X **to** Y.
- P ⇒ Q: contrapositive valid; converse not.
- Alphabet position A=1…Z=26; opposite sum 27.

## 2. Top 20 Traps
1. **Percentage base:** "A, B se 25% zyada" ⇒ B, A se 20% kam (base badalta hai).
2. Profit% CP par, **SP par nahi**.
3. **x% profit + x% loss** ≠ no gain — loss hai.
4. Average speed ≠ simple average (equal distance).
5. Unit conversion km/h ↔ m/s; **train ka length** bhoolna.
6. Work: rate = 1/time; **saath me time ≠ sum of times.**
7. Clock: 12 ghante me 11 overlaps (12 nahi).
8. Quadratic: D < 0 ⇒ complex; sum = −**b**/a.
9. AP: n-th term aur sum ka farak; terms count off-by-one (l − a)/d + 1.
10. Circular arrangement (n − 1)!, linear n!.
11. Repeated letters ⇒ divide.
12. **Pie/percentage points vs percent.**
13. DI: axis scale/units (lakh, %).
14. Cube painting: (n − 2) factor.
15. Similar figures: area = square, volume = cube.
16. Subject-verb: nearest noun ki jagah **head noun**; neither…nor ⇒ nearest.
17. **Since (point) vs for (period)**.
18. Double comparative, "prefer than".
19. Passage: **outside knowledge**/extreme words.
20. Syllogism: "some" ⇒ converse ok; "all" ⇒ converse nahi.

## 3. Time strategy (15 marks, ~15 min)
- **1-mark (5):** 45–60 s each — direct formula, grammar, vocabulary.
- **2-mark (5):** ≤ 2 min — RC, DI, work/speed, puzzles.
- **Pehle easy verbal/quant** (bulk secure), difficult puzzles last.
- **Negative marking:** 1-mark: −1/3; 2-mark: −2/3 (MCQ). **Eliminate 2 options** to guess.
- **Approximation:** DI/percent me rounding; options door hote hain.
- **Roz 20 min GA daily** (previous year GA, timer se): compounding se 8–10/15 easily.

## 4. Priority order (practice on web app)
1. **Quant — Percentage, Ratio, Profit-Loss, Time-Work, Speed** (highest frequency).
2. **Verbal — Grammar, Sentence ordering, Synonyms/Antonyms, RC.**
3. **Analytical — Seating, Direction, Series, Logical reasoning.**
4. **Geometry, DI, P&C/Probability.**
5. **Spatial (paper folding, cubes, mirror)** — recent years me kaafi.

- Syllogism/conditional logic, direction Pythagoras.
