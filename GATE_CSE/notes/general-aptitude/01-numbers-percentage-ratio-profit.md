## 1. Number System

- **Divisibility:** 2 (last digit even), 3 (digit sum ÷ 3), 4 (last 2 digits), 5, 8 (last 3), 9 (digit sum ÷ 9), **11 (alternate digit sum ka difference ÷ 11)**.
- **Prime:** sirf 1 aur khud se divisible; **2 sirf even prime**. 100 tak 25 primes.
- **Factors:** N = p^a q^b r^c ⇒ **factors count = (a+1)(b+1)(c+1)**; sum = Π (p^(a+1) − 1)/(p − 1). **Perfect square ⟺ sab powers even ⟺ factors odd.**
- **HCF × LCM = a × b** (do numbers). HCF: common factor; LCM: sab ke multiple.
- **Unit digit cycles:** 2: 2,4,8,6; 3: 3,9,7,1; 7: 7,9,3,1; 8: 8,4,2,6; 4: 4,6; 9: 9,1; 0,1,5,6 fixed. Power ko 4 se mod lo.
- **Remainder:** (a·b) mod n = (a mod n)(b mod n) mod n. **aⁿ + bⁿ, n odd ⇒ (a+b) se divisible; aⁿ − bⁿ ⇒ (a−b) se.**
- **Trailing zeros of n!** = ⌊n/5⌋ + ⌊n/25⌋ + …; 100! me 24.
- **Consecutive numbers:** n(n+1) even; n(n+1)(n+2) ÷ 6.
- **Fractions:** decimal terminating ⟺ denominator sirf 2, 5 factors. Compare: cross multiply.

**Example:** 2⁵⁰ ka unit digit: 50 mod 4 = 2 ⇒ 2² → **4**.
**Example:** 360 = 2³·3²·5 ⇒ factors 4·3·2 = **24**.
**Example:** 3-digit numbers jo 7 se divisible: ⌊999/7⌋ − ⌊99/7⌋ = 142 − 14 = **128**.
**Example:** 100 se 200 ke beech primes: 101,103,107,109,113,127,131,137,139,149,151,157,163,167,173,179,181,191,193,197,199 = **21**.

## 2. Powers, Roots, Logs, Absolute value
- aᵐaⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ; (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ; a⁰ = 1; a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ; a^(1/n) = ⁿ√a.
- **log_a(xy) = log x + log y; log(x/y) = log x − log y; log xⁿ = n log x; log_a b = 1/log_b a; log_a a = 1; log 1 = 0.** Domain: x > 0.
- **|x| = x (x ≥ 0), −x (x < 0).** |x| < a ⟺ −a < x < a; |x| > a ⟺ x > a ya x < −a. |x − a| = distance.
- **Surds:** √2 ≈ 1.414, √3 ≈ 1.732, √5 ≈ 2.236; π ≈ 3.14; e ≈ 2.718.
**Example:** 2^x = 32 ⇒ x = 5. log₂ 8 = 3. log 100 (base 10) = 2. |2x − 3| < 5 ⇒ −1 < x < 4.

## 3. Percentage
- **x% of y = xy/100 = y% of x.** **Percentage change = (New − Old)/Old × 100.**
- **Successive change:** +a% phir +b% ⇒ **a + b + ab/100**. **+20% phir −20% ⇒ −4%.**
- **Fraction–percent:** 1/2 = 50; 1/3 = 33.33; 1/4 = 25; 1/5 = 20; 1/6 = 16.67; 1/7 ≈ 14.28; 1/8 = 12.5; 1/9 = 11.11; 1/10 = 10; 1/11 = 9.09; 1/12 = 8.33.
- **Price up p% ⇒ consumption kitni ghatayein taaki kharch same:** p/(100 + p) × 100. **Price 25% ↑ ⇒ consumption 20% ↓.**
- **Population growth:** P(1 + r/100)ⁿ.
- **Salary/ marks:** pass % questions: pass marks = x% of total.
**Example:** Rate 20% badha; kharch same ⇒ consumption 16.67% kam.
**Example:** Length 10% ↑, width 10% ↑ ⇒ area 21% ↑. **Radius 10% ↑ ⇒ area 21% ↑.**
**Example:** A ki income B se 25% zyada ⇒ B ki income A se **20%** kam.

## 4. Ratio & Proportion
- **a : b = a/b.** Divide N in ratio a:b: N·a/(a+b), N·b/(a+b).
- **Proportion:** a/b = c/d ⇒ ad = bc. **Componendo-dividendo:** (a+b)/(a−b) = (c+d)/(c−d).
- **Direct** (y ∝ x), **inverse** (y ∝ 1/x). **Compound ratio** multiply.
- **Mean proportion** √(ab); **third proportion** b²/a.
- **Partnership:** profit ∝ capital × time.
**Example:** A:B = 2:3, B:C = 4:5 ⇒ A:B:C = 8:12:15.
**Example:** A, B, C capital 2000, 3000, 5000 (same time), profit 1000 ⇒ 200, 300, 500.
**Example:** A ₹3000 for 6 months, B ₹4000 for 3 months: 18000 : 12000 = 3 : 2.

## 5. Average
- **Average = sum/count.** Sum = avg × n. **Consecutive n numbers ka average = middle.** Arithmetic progression avg = (first + last)/2.
- **Weighted average** = Σ(wᵢxᵢ)/Σwᵢ.
- **Naya member add karne par:** new avg = (old sum + x)/(n+1); agar naya member avg se d zyada, avg badhta d/(n+1).
- **Average speed (equal distance) = 2ab/(a+b)** (harmonic), (equal time) = (a+b)/2.
**Example:** 5 numbers ka avg 20; ek hataya, avg 18 ⇒ hataya number = 100 − 72 = **28**.
**Example:** 60 km/h aana, 40 km/h jaana (same distance): avg = 2·60·40/100 = **48**.
**Example:** 1 se 100 ka avg = 50.5.

## 6. Profit & Loss
- **Profit = SP − CP; Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100** (hamesha CP par). **SP = CP(1 ± %/100).**
- **Marked price (MP), discount:** SP = MP(1 − d/100). **Successive discount: a + b − ab/100.** 20% + 10% ⇒ 28%.
- **Same SP par x% profit aur x% loss ⇒ hamesha loss = x²/100 %.**
- **False weight:** profit% = (true − false)/false × 100.
- **Cost price kaise nikaale:** 25% profit par SP 500 ⇒ CP = 500/1.25 = 400.
**Example:** CP 200, MP 300, 20% discount: SP 240; profit 20%.
**Example:** 900 gram ko 1 kg bata ke bechna, cost price par: profit = 100/900 = 11.11%.
**Example:** 2 items 600 each, ek 20% profit ek 20% loss ⇒ net **loss 4%** (x²/100).

## 7. Simple aur Compound Interest
- **SI = PRT/100.** A = P + SI.
- **CI: A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ.** Half-yearly: r/2, 2n. Quarterly: r/4, 4n.
- **CI − SI (2 saal) = P(r/100)².** **SI se CI zyada jab T > 1.**
- **Rule of 72:** doubling time ≈ 72/r saal.
**Example:** ₹10,000, 10% CI, 2 saal: 10000(1.21) = 12,100; CI = 2100 vs SI 2000.
**Example:** ₹5000 at 8% SI 3 saal: 1200.
**Example:** Kaunsa zyada: 10% quarterly vs 10.5% annual? Quarterly effective (1.025)⁴ − 1 = 10.38% < 10.5%.

## 8. Alligation & Mixture
- **Alligation rule:** cheap : dear = (d − m) : (m − c). d = dear price, c = cheap, m = mean.
- **Replacement:** container me V litre, x litre nikaal ke replace, n baar: **final = V(1 − x/V)ⁿ.**
**Example:** ₹20/kg aur ₹30/kg chawal mix ₹24/kg: ratio (30−24):(24−20) = 6:4 = **3:2**.
**Example:** 40 L milk se 4 L nikal ke water daala, 2 baar: 40(0.9)² = 32.4 L milk bacha.
**Example:** 60% aur 20% acid solution mix 40%: ratio 1:1.

## 10. Quick Revision
- % change = Δ/old; +a, +b ⇒ a + b + ab/100.
- Profit% on CP; same SP x% profit/loss ⇒ loss x²/100 %.
- Factors count (a+1)(b+1); HCF×LCM = product.
- Avg speed equal distance = 2ab/(a+b).
- CI = P(1+r)ⁿ − P; alligation cross method.
