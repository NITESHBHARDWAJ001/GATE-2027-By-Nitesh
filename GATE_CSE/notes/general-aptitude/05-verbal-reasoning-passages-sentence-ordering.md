## 1. Reading Comprehension (RC)

GATE me passage chhota (100–250 words) hota hai, 1–2 questions.

### Strategy
1. **Pehle questions padho** (kya poocha ja raha), phir passage.
2. **Main idea:** pehla aur aakhri paragraph, topic sentences.
3. **Tone/attitude:** critical, supportive, neutral, sarcastic — adjectives se pakdo.
4. **Har option ko passage se verify karo — apni knowledge use mat karo.**
5. **Extreme words (always, never, only, all, completely)** wale options aksar galat.
6. **"Inference" ka matlab:** passage se logically nikle; **assumption** ≠ inference; **not directly stated.**

### Question types
| Type | Kaise |
|---|---|
| Main idea/title | Poore passage ka central point, narrow detail nahi |
| Detail | Line dhoondo, paraphrase match |
| Inference | Ek step aage, par passage ke andar |
| Vocabulary in context | Aas-paas ki lines se meaning |
| Author's tone | Words ka charge |
| Except/Not true | Har option check, ek galat |

**Example:** Passage: "Rapid urbanisation has strained water supply, yet policy focuses on new infrastructure instead of conservation." Main idea: **Policy neglects conservation despite urban water stress.** "Cities have enough water" ⇒ galat (contradicts).
**Example (extreme word):** "All scientists agree…" jab passage me "many" ho ⇒ galat.

## 2. Sentence Ordering / Paragraph Jumble (Narrative Sequencing)

4–5 sentences shuffled; sahi order chuno (ya sentence insert).

### Technique
1. **Opening sentence dhundo:** general/introduction, koi pronoun (he, this, they) na ho jiska referent pehle na aaya ho.
2. **Pronoun/reference chain:** "It", "This", "However", "Thus" pehle kuch mangte hain.
3. **Connectors:** however/but (contrast), therefore/thus (result), moreover/also (addition), for example (illustration), finally (last).
4. **Chronology:** cause → effect; general → specific; problem → solution.
5. **Pairs dhundo (mandatory pairs)** aur option elimination.
6. **Article "a" pehle, "the" baad me** (first mention vs already known).
**Example (order):**
P: However, the results were inconsistent.
Q: Researchers tested a new drug on mice.
R: They hoped to see faster recovery.
S: Larger trials are therefore needed.
Order: **Q R P S** (intro → aim → contrast → conclusion).
**Example (insertion):** Missing sentence connector se dekho — pehle/baad ke sentence se logic.

## 3. Verbal Reasoning / Logical inference (verbal)

- **Statement–Conclusion:** conclusion tabhi sahi jab statement se **certainly** follow kare.
- **Assumption / conclusion:** Assumption = hidden premise; conclusion = jo nikle.
- **Sufficiency ("Statement I alone sufficient?")** — data sufficiency style: har statement alag se check, phir combine.
- **Strengthen / weaken:** premise ko support ya todne wala.

### Syllogism (Venn se)
- **All A are B; All B are C ⇒ All A are C.**
- **Some A are B ⇒ Some B are A** (converse valid).
- **All A are B ⇒ Some B are A (definite for existence)** — GATE me "some" aksar "at least one".
- **No A is B ⇒ No B is A.**
- **All A are B does NOT give All B are A.**
- **Some A are not B** — reverse nahi.
- **Two "some" premises ⇒ koi conclusion nahi.** **Two negatives ⇒ no conclusion.**
**Example:** All engineers are logical. Some logical people are artists. ⇒ "Some engineers are artists" **follows nahi** (⇒ possible, not certain).
**Example:** All cats are animals; All animals breathe ⇒ **All cats breathe.**

### "Statements follow"
1. Statement ke sirf literal claims se conclusion test.
2. **Outside knowledge nahi.**
3. **Negation check:** conclusion ko negate karke dekho contradiction aata hai kya.
**Example:** "No student passed without studying. Ravi passed." ⇒ **Ravi studied.** (follows).
**Example:** "Ravi studied." ⇒ Ravi passed? **No** (necessary ≠ sufficient).

### Necessary vs Sufficient
- "**If P then Q**" (P ⇒ Q): P sufficient, Q necessary. **Contrapositive ¬Q ⇒ ¬P valid; converse Q ⇒ P invalid.**
- "**Only if**": "P only if Q" = P ⇒ Q. "**Unless**": "P unless Q" = ¬Q ⇒ P.
**Example:** "He will pass only if he studies." ⇒ pass ⇒ studies. Studies ⇏ pass.

## 4. Data Sufficiency (verbal)
- Question ka answer nikalne ke liye kaunsa statement kaafi:
  (I alone / II alone / both together / dono alag-alag / neither).
- **Pehle question ko simplify karo, phir I, phir II, phir I + II.**
**Example:** "Kya x > y?" I: x = 5. II: y = 3. Alone koi nahi; **together x > y (5 > 3)** ⇒ dono chahiye.

## 5. GATE patterns
1. **Reading comprehension** (main idea / inference) – 2 marks.
2. **Sentence ordering / narrative sequencing.**
3. **Statements-follow / logical conclusion.**
4. **Statement sufficiency.**
5. **Author tone/paragraph title.**

## 6. Quick Revision
- Passage: options ko passage se match; extreme words galat.
- Sentence order: opening (no pronoun), connectors, pairs, chronology.
- Syllogism: Venn; two "some" ⇒ nothing.
- P ⇒ Q: contrapositive ok, converse nahi.
- Inference = passage se nikla; assumption = chhupi shart.
- Sufficiency: I, II, phir dono.

### Practice
1. "All pens are ink-based; some ink-based are red." Conclusion "some pens are red"? *(Follow nahi)*
2. "Only if" translate: "You get a seat only if you register." *(seat ⇒ registered)*
3. Order: (P) Therefore, ban plastic. (Q) Plastic pollutes oceans. (R) Marine life suffers. *(Q R P)*
